## I. Index Notes

This index covers the principal legal documents currently in force in the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization and their core provisions, arranged by legal hierarchy and department. The scope of the index includes the Symbiotic Constitution, the Symbiotic Labor Law, the Consumer Protection Law, and the five supporting laws on education, medical care, housing, environment, and antitrust. Each entry lists the name of the law, the provision number, and a summary of the core content of the provision. This index is a reference tool; for the complete text of the legal provisions and judicial interpretations, refer to the official texts of the respective laws.

## III. The Symbiotic Labor Law

The Symbiotic Labor Law is the basic law regulating labor relations and protecting the rights and interests of workers, enacted on the legislative basis of Article 42 of the Symbiotic Constitution.

| Provision No. | Core Content |
|----------|----------|
| Article 1 | Legislative purpose: to regulate labor relations, protect the lawful rights and interests of workers, and build harmonious and symbiotic labor relations |
| Article 2 | Scope of application: all labor relations within the Hengyuan Universe, including material labor, cultivation labor, and creative labor |
| Article 3 | Establishes the principle of equal employment of workers; employment discrimination based on race, gender, cultivation level, or origin is prohibited |
| Article 4 | Establishes the principle of free choice of employment; forced labor and slavery are prohibited |
| Article 5 | Provides for the labor contract system; a written labor contract shall be concluded when establishing a labor relationship |
| Article 6 | Provides for the mandatory clauses of a labor contract: term of the contract, work content, labor remuneration, working hours, labor protection, and social insurance |
| Article 7 | Establishes the eight-hour working day and the working hour system of no more than forty-four hours per week |
| Article 8 | Provides for overtime restrictions: overtime shall not exceed one hour per day, or three hours under special circumstances, and shall not exceed thirty-six hours per month |
| Article 9 | Provides for overtime wage standards: 1.5 times the wage for overtime on working days, 2 times the wage for overtime on rest days, and 3 times the wage for overtime on statutory holidays |
| Article 10 | Establishes the minimum wage guarantee system; the minimum wage standard is formulated by each star domain according to local conditions |
| Article 11 | Provides for the wage payment system: wages shall be paid monthly to the worker personally in monetary form, and shall not be withheld or delayed without cause |
| Article 12 | Establishes the distribution principle of more work, more gain; a distribution system with distribution according to work as the main body and multiple distribution methods coexisting is implemented |
| Article 13 | Provides for the labor safety and health system; employers must provide workers with labor safety and health conditions that meet the standards |
| Article 14 | Provides for the protection of special groups: special labor protection is implemented for minor workers, female workers, disabled workers, and workers with low cultivation levels |
| Article 15 | Establishes the vocational training system; employers have the obligation to provide vocational skills training for workers |
| Article 16 | Provides for the labor rights and interests of cultivators: cultivation labor is equally protected by this Law, and the ownership of cultivation results is agreed upon by contract |
| Article 17 | Establishes the social insurance system for workers: pension insurance, medical insurance, unemployment insurance, work-related injury insurance, and maternity insurance |
| Article 18 | Provides for the welfare system for workers: employers shall provide workers with necessary welfare benefits |
| Article 19 | Establishes the labor dispute handling system: a four-level handling mechanism of consultation, mediation, arbitration, and litigation |
| Article 20 | Provides for the trade union system; workers have the right to organize and join trade unions in accordance with the law to protect their lawful rights and interests |
| Article 21 | Provides for the collective contract system; trade unions represent workers in concluding collective contracts with employers |
| Article 22 | Provides that the rules and regulations of an employer shall be discussed by the workers' congress or all workers |
| Article 23 | Provides for the conditions and procedures for the termination of labor contracts |
| Article 24 | Provides for the economic compensation system; employers shall pay economic compensation when lawfully terminating labor contracts |
| Article 25 | Provides for the compensation system for unlawful termination of labor contracts |
| Article 26 | Establishes the labor dispatch management system, regulating labor dispatch employment |
| Article 27 | Provides for the special rules for part-time employment |
| Article 28 | Provides for the supervision and inspection system; labor administrative departments supervise and inspect the compliance of employers with labor laws and regulations in accordance with the law |
| Article 29 | Provides for legal liability: employers who violate this Law shall bear administrative, civil, and criminal liability in accordance with the law |
| Article 30 | Provides for the effective date and the power of interpretation of this Law |

## IV. The Consumer Protection Law

The Consumer Protection Law is the basic law protecting the lawful rights and interests of consumers and regulating the conduct of business operators.

| Provision No. | Core Content |
|----------|----------|
| Article 1 | Legislative purpose: to protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers, maintain the social economic order, and promote the healthy development of the symbiotic market economy |
| Article 2 | Scope of application: the protection of the rights and interests of consumers within the Hengyuan Universe when purchasing, using commodities or receiving services for the needs of daily life |
| Article 3 | Establishes the basic principles of transactions between business operators and consumers: voluntariness, equality, fairness, and good faith |
| Article 4 | Establishes the principle that the state protects the lawful rights and interests of consumers; the state takes measures to guarantee that consumers exercise their rights in accordance with the law |
| Article 5 | Provides for the nine basic rights of consumers: the right to safety, the right to information, the right to free choice, the right to fair dealing, the right to claim compensation, the right to association, the right to knowledge, the right to respect, and the right to supervision |
| Article 6 | Provides for the consumer's right to safety: consumers enjoy the right that their personal and property safety is not harmed when purchasing and using commodities and receiving services |
| Article 7 | Provides for the consumer's right to information: consumers enjoy the right to know the true information of the commodities they purchase or use and the services they receive |
| Article 8 | Provides for the consumer's right to free choice: consumers enjoy the right to freely choose commodities or services |
| Article 9 | Provides for the consumer's right to fair dealing: consumers enjoy the right to fair dealing and have the right to refuse coercive transactions |
| Article 10 | Provides for the consumer's right to claim compensation: consumers who suffer personal or property damage due to the purchase and use of commodities or the receipt of services enjoy the right to obtain compensation in accordance with the law |
| Article 11 | Provides for the basic obligations of business operators: to perform statutory and agreed obligations, accept supervision, ensure safety, provide true information, issue vouchers, guarantee quality, perform the three guarantees, refrain from unfair dealing, and refrain from infringing personal dignity |
| Article 12 | Provides for the safety guarantee obligation of business operators: business operators shall guarantee that the commodities or services they provide meet the requirements of protecting personal and property safety |
| Article 13 | Provides for the information disclosure obligation of business operators: business operators shall provide consumers with true information about the commodities or services, and shall not make false or misleading publicity |
| Article 14 | Provides for the special norms for cultivation products and services: business operators of cultivation methods, cultivation instruments, and energy products must possess special qualifications, and the products must pass safety certification |
| Article 15 | Provides for the regulation of standard terms: business operators shall not use standard terms to exclude or limit consumer rights, or to mitigate or exempt the operator's liability |
| Article 16 | Provides for the seven-day no-reason return system: consumers who purchase commodities through remote methods such as the network have the right to return the commodities within seven days from the date of receipt |
| Article 17 | Provides for the three guarantees system: if the commodities or services provided by business operators do not meet quality requirements, consumers may request repair, replacement, or return in accordance with national regulations |
| Article 18 | Provides for the punitive damages system: if business operators engage in fraudulent conduct in providing commodities or services, they shall, in accordance with the requirements of consumers, increase compensation for the losses suffered, with the increased compensation amount being three times the price of the commodities purchased or the cost of the services received by the consumer |
| Article 19 | Provides for consumer organizations: consumers' associations and other consumer organizations are social organizations lawfully established to carry out social supervision over commodities and services and to protect the lawful rights and interests of consumers |
| Article 20 | Provides for the channels for resolving consumer disputes: negotiation and settlement with business operators, requesting mediation by consumers' associations, appealing to relevant administrative departments, submitting to arbitration, and initiating litigation in court |
| Article 21 | Provides for the reversal of the burden of proof in disputes: for durable commodities or services such as decoration, if consumers discover defects within six months from the date of receipt, the business operator bears the burden of proof |
| Article 22 | Provides for the administrative and criminal liability of business operators |
| Article 23 | Provides for the effective date and the power of interpretation of this Law |

## Conclusion

The index of legal provisions is the reference map of the rule-of-law system of the Anchor Symbiotic Civilization. From the Symbiotic Constitution as the fundamental law, to the Labor Law and the Consumer Protection Law regulating people's livelihood, and then to the five supporting laws, together they constitute a complete legal system "with the Constitution as the commander, the basic laws as the backbone, and the supporting laws as the support". This system ensures that all activities of the symbiotic civilization operate on the track of the rule of law, providing a solid institutional guarantee for the common advancement of the Ten Thousand Races.
